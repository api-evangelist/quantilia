Analyzing financial risks is a crucial component of economic stability and decision-making for investors, regulators, and governments. This study examines the evolution of various financial risk measures, highlighting credit ratings from rating agencies, the VIX volatility index, the time it takes to sell an apartment in the real estate sector, and other relevant indicators. 1.
